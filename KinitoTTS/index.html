Tips
These are some tips for making the TTS sound more like KinitoPET!

If your TTS sounds more monotone/dry than KinitoPET ingame,
That means there isn't much exclamation marks.

Kinitos TTS used in game uses exclamation marks pretty often
in order to make Kinito sound more excited.

Make sure not to use fully capitalized words.
Otherwise the TTS will try fully spelling out the word
instead of reading it.

If the TTS mispronounces a word or expands into a different one,
(Example being in turns into inches and thur turns into Thursday)

Try to change the spelling of the word in a way to give more
accurate pronounciation, like sounding it out.
(Changing in to inn or thur to thurr)
